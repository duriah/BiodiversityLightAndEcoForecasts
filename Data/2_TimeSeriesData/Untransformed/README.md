# Data information

- `AbundanceTimeSeries`: the density (individuals per ml) and biomass (grams per ml) time series of the different taxa.
- `CarbonNitrogenTimeSeries.csv`: the time series of  DC (Dissolved Carbon), DN (Dissolved Nitrogen), DOC (Dissolved Organic Carbon) and IC (Inorganic Carbon). All units are mg per L. 
- `OxygenTimeSeries.csv`: the oxygen time series for the two sensors 4 and 9 (numbers reflect the positional height of the sensors within the experimental units). Oxygen concentration is given in percent.
